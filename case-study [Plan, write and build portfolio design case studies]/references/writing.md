# Writing

## Voice

- Neutral and professional by default; first person for decisions ("I led…", "we tested…").
- No self-praise. No hype adjectives. Let the decision and its reason do the work.
- Problem first, then how the project answers it, then a plain tools list.
- Mixed teams: "I" for your parts, "we" for shared ones, names for theirs.

## Sentence patterns

- **Finding → decision.** "71 % of pilot sessions started on 'who's free now', so it became the
  home screen's first block."
- **What didn't change, and why.** "The timetable logic survived the pivot untouched; only the
  visual language changed." Restraint is evidence of judgement.
- **Principle in one sentence**, then everything follows from it ("Album artwork is part of the
  UI, not a standalone visual").
- **Headline states the finding with its number** ("Why the 68 % stopped trying"), not a topic
  label ("Survey results").
- **Constraint voice:** "The research set three constraints the app had to work inside."
- **Honest status line** near the top when something wasn't tested or built for real.

## Captions

Caption = the insight. Read alone, the captions should retell the story.

## Cut

- Tool walk-throughs (one caption covers them).
- Paragraphs that repeat what a chart already says (cut the paragraph, not the chart).
- Methodology repeated per section: state the cohort once at the top of results.
- Generic artefacts with no consequence (empathy maps, personas, journey maps that lead to no
  decision).
- Anything that makes this page much longer than the others in the portfolio.

## Learnings and ending

- At most three, specific, honest ("I still consider myself a beginner in these tools").
- Open questions are allowed and read as confidence (Mercury: "So, what's next? I have no clue").
- Next steps concrete (Calmsphere: pilot test, next prototype, electronics and enclosure).
- Credit contributors by name.

## Bilingual pages

Translate every new string; check that the translation wraps to the same number of lines as the
original (measure rendered line counts, not character counts) and shorten until it does.
