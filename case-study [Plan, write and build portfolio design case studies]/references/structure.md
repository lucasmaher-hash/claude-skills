# Structure

## Canonical order

Overview → problem → role → research & insights → process → key decisions with reasons → final
solution → result. As a page skeleton, ~900–1,100 words of prose in total. Every section must
survive being cut to two paragraphs.

| # | Section | Job (one only) | Typical visual |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hero + one-liner** | The 30-second version: problem and answer in one sentence | Product in use, ideally moving; or the product inside the title (Calmsphere) |
| 2 | **Overview block + meta** | Five answers before any process image | Label/value rows; timeline · role · team · platform/tools · status |
| 3 | **Problem** | Strongest paragraph on the page, with its source and how solid it is | A number, a recreated real artefact (chat, desk, footage) |
| 4 | **Research → one model** | Condense findings into a model that forces the next decision | Spectrum, axis, options table, funnel |
| 5 | **The one idea** | The insight everything else follows from, plus the system in a few lines | Mechanism diagram, wireframes |
| 6 | **The key decision / pivot** | Most space: what it was, why it went, what replaced it, what survived | Before/after, same screens, same crop |
| 7 | **System & craft** | 2–3 hard, invisible details. Short, proven, no lecture | Detail triplets, motion of the interaction |
| 8 | **Accessibility** (if real) | Measured work: rare in junior portfolios, strong signal | Numbers: contrast ratios, Dynamic Type sizes, audit fixes |
| 9 | **Result & learnings** | Where honesty lands: what happened, setbacks included, ≤3 lessons, next steps | Results chart, before/after numbers, launch video |

### The overview block (from Calmsphere)

Directly under the hero, five bold-labelled lines:

```
What is it?          One sentence, product + what it does.
User problem:        One sentence.
My role:             Exact. In a team: name each person's part.
What I designed:     The concrete deliverables (form, interaction system, app…).
Outcome/validation:  What actually happened or was measured. If nothing yet, say "concept,
                     not yet tested" rather than something vague.
```

It replaces a vague "At a glance" paragraph and answers a recruiter's first five questions
before they scroll.

### Fact bar

2–4 large numbers, one line of context each, plus source and timeframe (Jessica Im: "data from
the first month after launch"). A number describing the **problem** ("17 % drop off", "20
questions instead of 3") is often stronger than one celebrating the result. No metrics →
verifiable facts in the same visual form.

## Section rules learned the hard way

- **One job per section.** If two sections both show the final screens, one is redundant. If the
  rejected v1 appears in "The idea" and again in "The pivot", show it once, as the comparison.
- **Proof belongs at the end.** Results data before the reader knows how it was built reads
  chronologically backwards. Keep pilot numbers out of the idea section.
- **No standalone design-system / colour / type section** unless the design system IS the
  deliverable. Fold it into the pivot as the *reason* the look changed (a paragraph with two
  inline swatches beat three specimen cards).
- **Say the structure up front** (Yuan: "I went in three steps") or give a section nav (sticky
  on desktop only, hidden on phones).
- **Order features by measured use or by the user's path through the product**, not by the
  app's tab order or the build order.
- Section titles: short nouns ("The problem", "The constraints"). Subheads: full declarative
  sentences that state the finding ("Why the 68 % stopped trying").
- Constraints found by research read as "had to" ("the app had to work inside…"), not as
  decisions the designer wrote.

## Variants by project type

| Type | Weight shifts to |
|---|---|
| Shipped product | Results, post-launch failure and iteration, store/usage data |
| Team course project | Role precision, team decision process, pivot evidence |
| Speculative concept (Mercury OS) | A guiding metaphor, own vocabulary small→large, dead ends, open questions |
| Industrial design + app (Calmsphere) | Hardware and UI as ONE product: sketch → CAD → render → UI, product in hand |
| Brand / visual (Finy) | One brand idea in a sentence, every element traced back to it, ~150 words |
| Media-led product (YesCheff) | Organised by the user's path; per feature: short paragraph + demo video + 3 labelled details |
